# Code of Conduct

This project is a place for careful, good-faith work on a mathematical result. Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests or discussions is expected to:

- **Be respectful.** Criticise claims, proofs and code, not people.
- **Credit others.** Attribute ideas, computations and code to their authors. Never present someone else's work as your own.
- **Be honest about evidence.** Say what you checked and what you did not, and report negative results and mistakes openly.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not post other people's personal information, and do not publish your own by accident.
- **Assume good faith.** Assume it until there is clear reason not to.

**Unacceptable behaviour.** Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and deliberate misattribution are not acceptable.

**Enforcement.** The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions that break this code, and may block participants who repeatedly do so. To raise a concern privately, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the *Security* tab and say that your report concerns conduct.
